xESS — build sheet

The handheld beside the interior map. Same vocabulary as the ePADD, one third of the width: 640 × 1080 at a 1920 console, the map taking the rest.

Two bands, not three

Identity bar — name, job, where you are, ARMED 56px, fixed
App area — the home tiles, or one app using the lot the rest. ONE region

The previous sheet had a third band pinned to the bottom holding the scene's choices and Beam up. It is gone. Every surface is an app, so the device has one region that changes shape instead of two that have to agree with a reserve row — and there is no arithmetic left to get wrong.

HAIL and PARTY were one app. One held three abstract audience tokens and the other held the actual people. Merged, the roster IS the inbox, grouped by who said it — and the ship is a row like anyone else, carrying BEAM UP, because the ship is what beams you up.

Calling one person by name is NEW. message_send addresses a CONSOLE, and every boarded console reports the same one, so two boarders are indistinguishable as addressees today. It needs a crew:<lifeform> audience — one branch in _audience_matches, which already takes client_id, plus boarding_client_of to invert it. Without it the roster lists people you cannot reach, which is worse than not listing them.

A tile is icon, name, blurb and a text badge — "3 here", "2 new", not a dot, because a dot cannot say how many. One column: 640px split four ways is 160px a tile, narrower than the words on it.

One entry and a count, never a thread. HAIL shows the newest message and says how many more are on the ePADD; SCAN shows this room and files the rest. That is what keeps the device off a scrollbar, and it is the same rule in both directions: the xESS acts, the ePADD reads.

Never a stack of gui_rows. A fixed row is never scaled down, so past what fits it spills out over the map — which is the bug the old choices band shipped with. The house container already handles wrapping, measurement and the scrollbar; assembling a substitute gets a worse version of one and hides the gap.

Three settings, a ladder

FULL is CUT's effect table with the damage figure raised, not a fourth mechanic. It earns its row by making lethality a NAMED CHOICE rather than something that falls out of pointing a cutting tool at a colleague — the same argument that puts the setting ahead of the target.

CUT and FULL only differ once something reads the setting. The library fires the shot and reports it; signal_emit("xess_fired", ...) already carries the setting, so a mission that wants "you needed that reactor intact" can say so. Until one does, the honest difference is the HP figure and the word in the identity bar.

Four things the code has to get right

A choice button carries its seq. boarding_answer re-derives the choice list and indexes into it, so an index captured when the button was built is only valid for that beat. Pass seq and agent in the button's data: a button from a superseded beat then answers nothing instead of answering the wrong thing.

A new beat opens ACT by itself, on every console at the site. Record the seq even when it does not open, or a beat somebody dismissed pops back on the next tick.

Except while armed. Yanking a crew member off an armed weapon screen is the exact accident the safety rules exist to prevent. Badge the tile instead.

Armed reaches the bar. The identity bar is the only thing on screen in every state, so it is where ARMED has to show.
